# S071 — Tightening nucleus top-p alongside temperature is redundant

- **Audit ID:** S071 (source issue #71)
- **Title:** Tightening nucleus top-p alongside temperature is redundant
- **Classification:** [ALG]
- **Severity:** low
- **Severity rationale:** Redundant but not incorrect; interaction is merely uncalibrated.

## Original defect description
'Tighten nucleus-top-p' alongside temperature is redundant: temperature scales the logits, top-p truncates the tail. Their interaction is non-linear and uncalibrated.

## Affected subsystem
S6 Thermodynamic Brake

## Root cause
Two sampling knobs were turned at once without studying their interaction.

## Proposed correction
Use temperature as the primary control in stations/s06_brake/controller.py; keep top-p fixed during control and document the fixed value in config/sampling.yaml; interaction study noted as future work.
